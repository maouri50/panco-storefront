# Panco Visual Direction — Warm Utility Study

The supplied leather-goods reference is being used solely to study high-level visual principles. Panco will retain its own wordmark, copy, product photography, page structure, and Cash on Delivery experience.

## Observed design principles to adapt

| Principle | Original Panco application |
| --- | --- |
| Warm tobacco leather imagery against soft bone surfaces | Use Panco’s existing product imagery with a parchment content background and richer chestnut image overlays. |
| Dark, restrained navigation over photography | Retain Panco navigation but use high-contrast ivory text on the hero and a compact dark-tobacco fallback surface while scrolling. |
| Sparse green utility treatment | Use a deep muted olive only for operational messages, Cash on Delivery assurance, and focused actions. |
| Crisp white sale and product surfaces | Use warm ivory cards and almost-black type, with thin aged-tan rules rather than copied component shapes. |
| Clear price hierarchy | Preserve Panco’s own price and sale data, with current price in dark ink and comparative price subdued. |

| `--panco-tobacco` | `#6f452f` | Leather-toned emphasis and image overlay |

The next implementation step is to apply these original tokens across public Panco pages while keeping accessible contrast and the existing responsive product and checkout behavior.
