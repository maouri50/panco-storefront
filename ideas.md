# North Atelier — Design Exploration

## Three possible directions

### 1. Patina Modernism
**Very Brief Intro:** An editorial leather house where deep forest, saddle brown, and natural paper support expressive type, off-kilter photo crops, and a feeling of workshop provenance.

**Probability:** 0.07

### 2. Gallery Utility
**Very Brief Intro:** A pale, architecture-led retail space built from bold scale, chalky mineral surfaces, and object-study photography that treats leather goods as small sculptures.

**Probability:** 0.04

### 3. Nocturne Field Notes
**Very Brief Intro:** A dark, cinematic field journal that pairs grainy night photography with brass accents and compact typographic annotations for a more adventurous product world.

**Probability:** 0.09

---

## Chosen Direction: Patina Modernism

### Reference Grounding
The reference site establishes a premium leather storefront through a restrained dark-green announcement bar, overlaid navigation, large photographic hero moments, serif display typography, micro-spaced uppercase labels, product cards, and a workshop-oriented provenance story. North Atelier will use the same broad category cues without duplicating its brand, exact layout, copy, or product catalog.

### Design Movement
**Contemporary craft editorial**, taking cues from independent fashion lookbooks, Japanese product catalogs, and material-led print design.

### Core Principles
1. **Material is the interface:** leather, paper, shadow, and worn brass guide every surface decision.
2. **Editorial asymmetry:** the composition uses generous side gutters, offset labels, and layered captions rather than centered template blocks.
3. **Quiet authority:** high-contrast typography and limited color do the work; no decorative excess, gradients, or artificial luxury signals.
4. **Tactile pace:** image movement and interactions are slow, small, and confident, echoing handwork rather than software spectacle.

### Color Philosophy
Warm unbleached paper (`#F2EBDD`) creates a natural canvas; **Atelier Green** (`#163B32`) grounds navigation and calls-to-action like aged botanical ink; saddle (`#9B532F`) and oxidized brass (`#B28A52`) appear only as material cues; charcoal (`#1D211E`) anchors legibility. This is a warm, low-chroma palette designed to feel collected rather than trend-driven.

### Layout Paradigm
The homepage unfolds like a maker's folio: a full-bleed opening plate, an annotated product rail, a tall split chapter for a material story, and an overlapping editorial field note. Content aligns to flexible vertical seams and offset edges—not a rigid centered grid.

### Signature Elements
1. Thin olive rule-lines and compact inventory-style labels.
2. A small compass-rose / split-arc mark rendered as a bold graphic symbol, repeated as a framing device.
3. Floating numbered notes (e.g. `01 — FIELD CARRY`) attached to imagery and collections.

### Interaction Philosophy
Navigation gains a paper-toned surface on scroll; product cards reveal a deliberate quick-add cue; the cart opens as a right-side paper drawer. CTAs invert from green to paper on hover, and every interactive element has a clear focused state.

### Animation
Use 180–260ms ease-out for buttons and menus; image crops subtly scale to 1.025 on hover over 700ms; content lines enter with short opacity/translate sequences (40ms stagger) only on initial load. Respect reduced-motion settings. No bouncing, spinning, parallax, or continuous decorative motion.

### Typography System
**Cormorant Garamond** carries display headlines in regular and italic for an editorial, slightly human voice. **DM Mono** handles product data, buttons, labels, and navigation with compact uppercase tracking. Headlines are large, closely tracked, and asymmetrically positioned; body copy remains calm and readable at 15–17px.

### Brand Essence
**North Atelier creates hand-finished leather companions for people who keep going.** Personality: **considered, resilient, elemental**.

### Brand Voice
Headlines speak with concise material confidence; CTAs are direct and specific; microcopy identifies purpose and origin rather than making vague claims.

Example lines: “Made for the long way home.” and “Choose your everyday companion.”

### Wordmark & Logo
Use a custom `N/A` wordmark with compressed high-contrast serif letterforms, paired with a bold **north notch** symbol: two offset half-arcs divided by a vertical cut, suggesting a compass, a saddle stitch, and an open path. The symbol must be used prominently in the header and as the favicon.

### Signature Brand Color
**Atelier Green — `#163B32`**.

## Style Decisions

- Product imagery must not show third-party logos or recognizable outside branding; every object must read as part of the North Atelier house catalog.
- The `N/A` wordmark and north-notch symbol are the primary brand signature: they appear prominently in the first viewport, in the global header, and as recurring framing details.
- Commerce remains folio-like rather than template-like: numbered references, thin rules, material annotations, and deliberately offset objects make product information feel collected and editorial.
