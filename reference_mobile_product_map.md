# Mobile Product Reference Map

## Tiles 01–02

The mobile product experience opens with a narrow dark utility announcement, a light icon-led navigation strip, then a spacious title/price ledger. The title sits left of the current and previous price, with a compact rating line below. A large, almost square product image follows immediately, presented on a pale neutral field with generous whitespace around the object.

North Atelier will translate this into an original compact top bar, icon navigation, product title and price ledger, and a tall primary object-study image. It will not reproduce the referenced retailer’s brand mark, pricing, rating content, or customer-generated material.

## Tiles 03–04

Below the primary image, the reference uses centered pagination marks, a horizontal thumbnail strip, a restrained stock-status label, then large image-backed color choices. The next control cluster separates stock action from communication and places a bordered quick-order card immediately beneath the primary choice.

North Atelier will use a compact carousel indicator, horizontal gallery thumbnails, original color selection cards, an available-for-dispatch status rather than copied stock language, and a bordered Cash on Delivery request card. The existing order form will remain original and will never imply that a product is unavailable unless that is configured in the catalog.

## Tiles 05–06

The quick-order card continues with paired personal-detail fields, then full-width address and city fields, ending in a large high-contrast order button. Product copy begins immediately after the card with a concise product promise, followed by easy-to-scan descriptive paragraphs and a clearly labeled specifications list.

North Atelier will keep its secure order submission but present it as a single soft-shadowed Cash on Delivery card. The object description will follow as a mobile reading block, with original material highlights presented as a direct checklist rather than copied specifications text.

## Tiles 07–08

The information section resolves into a quiet stack of full-width accordion rows with generous white space, hairline separators, spaced uppercase labels, and a plus icon aligned at the far right. The next section begins after a deliberately large visual pause.

North Atelier will retain its original materials, marking, care, and delivery content but use this more compact mobile accordion rhythm. The review area will begin after a clear section break and will use only a neutral empty-state prompt until genuine customer feedback exists.

## Tiles 09–10

The reference gives customer feedback its own generously spaced module: centered title, a rating summary, horizontal distribution rows, and a bold review action with an empty-state message below. Although the page displays rating visuals, it also states that no approved reviews exist.

North Atelier will use a **no reviews yet** section with a contact or notes action, but will not render star ratings, distributions, testimonials, or other fabricated customer signals. The high-contrast central action and open space can be retained without making unsubstantiated review claims.

## Tiles 11–12

The related-items module restarts on a light field with a small category label, a large centered heading, and a simple single-column product card. The product card keeps the object image prominent, then places uppercase product naming, a compact color mark, and price beneath before the dark footer begins.

North Atelier will keep related items as a small mobile carousel or single-column card sequence, using its own catalog data and available product imagery. The footer will begin as a strong dark chapter break, keeping the neutral ledger palette rather than the reference’s branding and exact details.

## Tiles 13–14

The footer starts with a small house mark, a concise studio statement, and a language selector, then shifts into a long, single-column exploration list with generous vertical spacing. The content is monochrome and intentionally quiet, letting the footer feel like a final editorial page rather than a dense link utility.

North Atelier will preserve its own studio statement, locale selector, and original navigation labels while adopting this mobile-first dark footer pacing.

## Tiles 15–16

The lower footer adds a compact social group, a large single-purpose newsletter invitation, a rounded email field with a circular action, a concise privacy line, and a final low-contrast copyright rule.

North Atelier will retain its existing newsletter form and original links, but restyle the mobile footer to use this more focused single-column cadence and clear visual close.

## Sale-layout purchase pattern

The supplied sale reference replaces a conventional product ledger with a storefront-like purchase system: an olive delivery notice; a pale blush icon header; a direct availability line; image-backed color cards; and a large, rounded **Easy Order** card. The card presents two compact personal-information fields, full-width address and city fields, and a single prominent green confirmation action. The page uses generous vertical breathing room, rounded input outlines, and dark technical uppercase labels.

North Atelier will translate this as an original purchase module with its existing catalog variants, own product imagery, and secure Cash on Delivery procedure. The outcome will not copy the reference brand mark, exact text, or external customer content.
