# Sahara Wallet Product-Page Reference Analysis

The inspected reference uses a product-detail structure that is useful as an interaction and layout benchmark, while Panco will retain its own imagery, language, pricing, branding, and catalog content.

## Layout and Interaction Findings

| Area | Observed reference pattern | Original Panco adaptation |
|---|---|---|
| Gallery | A long sequence of product views appears before supporting product information, with thumbnail-style navigation available in the layout. | Retain Panco’s left thumbnail rail and selected-image stage, then support a fuller image sequence only when original Panco assets are available. |
| Product ledger | Breadcrumb, title, price, availability, color selections, then purchasing controls are presented in a compact order. | Keep the existing Panco right-side ledger, strengthen its spacing and maintain Panco’s original product metadata. |
| Color selection | Image-backed color cards change the purchasable variation. | Preserve Panco’s image-backed variant cards and selected-state treatment. |
| Order form | A clear four-field Cash on Delivery form follows the product selection: name, phone, address, and city. | Retain Panco’s tested four-field Cash on Delivery card and green confirmation action. |
| Supporting content | Description and material/guarantee disclosures follow the order form. | Maintain Panco’s original material and support content below the completed purchase area. |
| Reviews | The reference exposes customer-review controls. | Preserve Panco’s honest Reviews empty state; do not add invented ratings, counts, or reviews. |

## Implementation Boundary

Panco will adopt only the general hierarchy, image-navigation behavior, and purchase-flow principles. It will not reuse the reference product imagery, product names, prices, reviews, slogans, or source branding.
