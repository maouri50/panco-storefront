# Panco Homepage Hero Verification

The recomposed homepage hero was inspected in the live development preview at desktop and phone widths using the verified original Panco Long Mile Duffle image.

| View | Confirmed hero behavior |
|---|---|
| Desktop, 1440 × 900 | The Duffle image fills the viewport with a legible dark left overlay. The Panco vector mark is centered in the header, compact Shop/Studio/Journal/Contact navigation remains at the left, search and bag utilities remain at the right, and the editorial headline, CTA, carousel indicator, and lower caption strip are all visible. |
| Mobile, 390 × 844 | The header keeps the centered Panco mark, menu trigger, search, and bag. The hero image crops to preserve the Duffle silhouette, the compact studio stamp appears below the navigation, and the title, supporting copy, CTA, and carousel indicator stay within the viewport without clipping. |

The hero uses original Panco product photography and Panco campaign copy; no reference text or imagery is used.
